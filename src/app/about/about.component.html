I'm a computer science graduate student focused on artificial intelligence, machine learning, and ML systems. My work sits at the intersection of research and engineering, with a strong emphasis on turning learning algorithms into systems people can trust and actually use.

I spend most of my time working on representation learning, large language models, and agent architectures. I'm especially drawn to reliability, verification, and alignment questions: how to make AI systems more transparent, reproducible, and accountable while still being practical to build and deploy.

My research background includes neural speech decoding and mitigating hallucinations in language models. Alongside that, I've built data-driven systems and retrieval-based architectures that connect models to structured knowledge and improve grounded behavior in real applications.

I enjoy working across the stack, from modeling ideas and training workflows to backend infrastructure, evaluation pipelines, and user-facing tools. I'm motivated by building systems that are technically strong, thoughtfully designed, and production-minded from the start.

Outside of work, I enjoy swimming, playing the piano, and solving Rubik's cubes, each of which reflects the same mix of structure, rhythm, and problem-solving that draws me to computer science.
